    About the show

    The First Shadow goes back to 1959 in Hawkins, Indiana, years before the series, and follows a teenage Henry Creel whose family is hoping for a fresh start. It's written by Kate Trefry from a story by the Duffer Brothers, Jack Thorne and Trefry, and directed by Stephen Daldry.

    It started in London's West End in 2023, opened on Broadway in April 2025, and won a Special Tony Award for its staging.
